# Sleeve first experience beta

Use the deployed site with five consenting listeners before widening promotion. This guide is ready for the maintainer to share; no invitations or feedback requests have been sent automatically.

## Include these histories

- Two people with less than six months of Last.fm history, including one who has just started logging plays.
- Two established Last.fm accounts, including one with a large history.
- One person who already has a Spotify history ZIP. Requesting a new ZIP is optional and not a prerequisite for trying Sleeve.

Include a phone and a desktop, and at least two browser families. Let each person choose whether optional AI writing is enabled before setup. Its disclosure must make the compact-summary/provider processing understandable.

## Tasks, without coaching

1. From the homepage, work out what Sleeve will show and how to bring your own listening into it.
2. Start setup. Explain what is currently happening, whether your plays are saved, and whether you can explore. On one large account, reload after progress is visibly saved and check that it continues.
3. Open Overview. Find one observation that is useful or surprising and follow it to the underlying artist or dates. For a short archive, check that missing history is explained.
4. Open Discover. Choose a record you would actually try, explain the connection, then save, pass or mark it already heard. Try Undo. Community opinions should help without obscuring the personal reason.
5. Reopen the browser and find your saved record and shelf choice. Locate Data & settings and the counting/AI controls.

## Record only what is needed

Ask for browser/device, approximate history size, the route where a problem occurred, the action, expected result and actual result. A cropped screenshot is useful if the listener wants to share one. Do not request their raw archive, credentials or full listening history. Capture times to usable Overview and first playable pick separately from all optional checks finishing.

For each recommendation, record whether the artist/record was already known, whether the connection was understandable, and whether the listener would try it. A Spotify search click is not proof they listened or liked the record. Saves, passes and Undo are local feedback, not a public community score.

## Fix before widening promotion

- Any lost import, broken resumption, inaccessible control, dead route, misleading count or unsupported AI claim displayed as fact.
- Setup that looks stuck or hides whether the listening record is already usable.
- Repeated recommendations that ignore known/hidden choices, unverifiable starting records, or late writing that moves a record while it is being chosen.
- Mobile overflow, unreadable labels, keyboard traps or motion that ignores reduced-motion settings.
- No user can explain a personal finding or choose a worthwhile next record without coaching.

Synthetic tests establish repeatable mechanics. This review is still needed to establish whether the product is clear and worthwhile for actual listeners. It does not require a public repository; publish the MIT source after the maintainer's final review and enable GitHub's private vulnerability reporting once it becomes public.
