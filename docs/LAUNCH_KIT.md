## One sentence

Sleeve turns your Last.fm history or Spotify export into a personal music record: what changed, what stayed, and what to play next.

## Short announcement

Your favorite artist was not always your favorite artist.

I built Sleeve to explore the history behind your music taste: first plays, lasting discoveries, forgotten records, month-by-month changes, and something worthwhile to play next.

It's free to try in your browser, with no Sleeve account. Bring an existing Last.fm username or a Spotify history ZIP, or open the demo right away. Imported history stays in your browser; optional AI writing uses a compact summary and can be switched off.

Try it: https://liner-c7u.pages.dev/
Source (MIT): https://github.com/owenawsong/liner

## Developer introduction

Sleeve is an open-source music history explorer built with plain JavaScript, browser-local archives, Cloudflare Pages Functions and an optional budgeted OpenRouter writer. The analysis engine, catalogue-backed discovery, prompts, evidence validators and hosting code are included under MIT.

The demo and local Spotify import work without API keys. Contributions around accessible charts, import recovery, recommendation quality and audio-data coverage are welcome.
